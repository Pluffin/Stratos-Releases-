## Stratos 0.1.0

First public build.

- Opening Range Breakout strategy for TopstepX futures accounts (default: MNQ)
- Web dashboard: strategy configs, start/stop, live state and trade history
- Risk guardrails: daily max loss, daily profit target, drawdown floor, ATR/structural stops, breakeven and trailing
- Optional auto-run scheduler
- TopstepX API key encrypted on your PC

**Install:** download `Stratos.Setup.0.1.0.exe` below and follow the [install guide](../../#-install).
You need a free license key: [request access](https://github.com/Pluffin/access-requests/issues/new?template=request-access.yml&project=Stratos%20(ORB%20trading%20bot%20for%20TopstepX)).
